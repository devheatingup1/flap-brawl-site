Flap Brawl

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

The short version: Flap Brawl does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal information. The game works offline, and everything it remembers stays on your device.

What the game stores on your device

To remember your choices between play sessions, Flap Brawl saves the following on your iPhone only:

  • The player name you type on the main menu (optional; up to 12 characters).
  • Your match settings: number of rivals, CPU skill, number of lives and selected map.
  • Whether sound is on or off.

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. You can change it at any time in the game, and it is removed when you delete the app.

What we do not collect

Flap Brawl does not make network connections during play. It does not include:

  • Accounts, sign-in or in-app purchases.
  • Advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • Analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • Any third-party service that collects data.

The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Apple

Flap Brawl is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may collect information about app downloads and, if you choose to share it with developers, app crash and usage data. That collection is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.

If you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems in the game, and we do not share it.

Children

Flap Brawl does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, when you visit, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. We do not add any analytics or tracking to these pages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
