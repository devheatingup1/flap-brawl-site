Reporting a bug

Including these details helps us find and fix the problem faster:

  • What happened, and what you expected to happen.
  • How to make it happen again, step by step, if you can.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings › General › About).
  • The map you were playing, and whether your phone was upright or sideways.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one.

Feedback and ideas

Tell us what you enjoy, what feels too hard or too easy, or what you'd like to see next. Use the same email address.

Quick fixes to try first

  • No sound? Check the sound button in the top-right corner of the game, and make sure your iPhone's volume is up.
  • Game acting strangely? Close Flap Brawl fully (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again.
